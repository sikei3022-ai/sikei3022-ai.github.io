Delete your account and data — Skride

Applies to the Skride app by SEKOR-FZCO (United Arab Emirates).

You can delete your Skride account and all data associated with it at any time. There are two ways to do this.

Option 1 — in the app (instant)

  1. Open the Skride app.
  2. Go to Account (profile tab).
  3. Tap “Delete account and all data”.
  4. Confirm. Your account and all associated data are permanently deleted immediately.

Option 2 — by email

Send an email from the address linked to your account to info@skride.ru with the subject “Delete my account”. We verify the request and delete your account and data within 30 days.

What data is deleted

  • Account data — your email/login and sign-in data;
  • Workout data — distances, times, pace, routes, personal records, goals;
  • Heart-rate readings you entered or recorded;
  • Exercise-machine console photos you submitted for recognition (these are not stored long-term and are removed);
  • Any data synced to our servers under your account.

Retention. After deletion we do not retain your personal workout data. Copies in encrypted backups are purged within 30 days. We may keep the minimum records strictly required by applicable law (e.g. basic transaction logs), after which they are also deleted.

Local data

Workouts stored only on your device are removed when you clear the app data or uninstall the app.

Contact

Questions about deletion: info@skride.ru — SEKOR-FZCO, United Arab Emirates. See also our Privacy Policy.
