Privacy Policy — Skride

Effective date: 24 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Skride mobile application and website (the “App”) processes, why, and with whom it may be shared. The operator of the App for users outside the Russian Federation is SEKOR-FZCO, a Free Zone Company registered in the United Arab Emirates (“we”, “us”). For any privacy-related question, contact us at info@skride.ru.

1. Data we process

  • Location data (GPS). When you start recording a workout, the App uses your device location to build the route and calculate distance, pace and time. To keep the track uninterrupted, recording may continue while the screen is off or the App is in the background (background location) — but only while a workout you started is running; it stops when you stop the workout. When no workout is running, location is not used.
  • Workout data. Distance, time, pace, activity type (running, cycling, elliptical), routes, personal records, goals, and heart-rate readings if you enter them or connect a sensor.
  • Account data. If you register to sync across devices and join the ranking — an identifier/login and technical sign-in data. Passwords are stored in a protected (hashed) form.
  • Exercise-machine console photos. If you use the console-recognition feature, the photo you take is sent to our server and to a text-recognition service to extract the numbers.
  • Technical data. The minimum information required to operate the service (for example, the fact of a request to the server). We do not use advertising identifiers and do not show ads.

2. Why we process data

  • to record workouts, build routes and show statistics;
  • to sync your data across devices (if you are signed in);
  • to build the ranking and runner levels;
  • to provide personalised AI-coach suggestions;
  • to recognise exercise-machine console readings from a photo.

The legal bases for processing are the performance of our agreement with you (providing the App’s features) and your consent (for optional features such as sign-in, cloud sync and photo recognition).

3. Data storage

Workouts may be stored locally on your device. If you enable synchronisation or sign in, data is also stored on our servers hosted with reputable cloud providers. We keep data no longer than necessary to operate the service.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your data. To operate certain features, data may be shared with the following processors:

  • Text-recognition service (Vision OCR) — processing the console photo to extract numbers;
  • AI assistant provider — processing text requests to the AI coach;
  • Hosting providers — hosting the App’s server and website.

Data is shared only to the extent necessary for the corresponding feature.

5. International transfers

Your data may be processed on servers located outside your country, including in the United Arab Emirates and other jurisdictions where our providers operate. We take reasonable measures to ensure an adequate level of protection for such transfers.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your data, its correction or deletion, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent. You can delete your account and its data directly in the App (Account → “Delete account and all data”), or by writing to info@skride.ru. Local data can be removed by clearing it in the App settings or uninstalling the App.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country) without the consent of a parent or legal guardian.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. The current version is always available at this address; the effective date is shown at the top of the page.
